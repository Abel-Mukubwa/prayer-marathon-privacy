Account Deletion Request Profile

To maintain a secure and trusted community space, we provide all intercessors using the Prayer Marathon app with the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated profile information.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Prayer Marathon account and wipe your records from our systems, please click the button below to submit a deletion request via email:

Request Account Deletion via Email

Please ensure you fill out your First Name, Last Name, and the exact Phone Number used during your registration so we can verify your identity profile. Deletion requests are processed within 3 business days.

Data Retention & Deletion Policy Matrix

Once your identity verification check is complete, our systems perform a secure purge of the following data layers:

  • Personal Identifiers Deleted Permanently: Your First Name, Last Name, registered PIN, and verified Phone Number string records are completely wiped from our infrastructure.
  • App Activity & Wallet Balance Deleted: All historical marathon setup parameters, local usage analytics, and active token wallet balances are permanently dropped and cannot be recovered.
  • Data Kept for Security Safeguards: To protect our platform from community holiness violations and policy bypass attempts, phone records tied to active administrative moderation constraints or permanent bans (persistent sanctuary ban registry) will be securely preserved to maintain sanctuary safety guidelines.
